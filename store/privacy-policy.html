Privacy Policy

WhatColor - Color Picker & Eyedropper
Last updated: August 10, 2026

WhatColor - Color Picker & Eyedropper (“the Extension”) is a privacy-first browser extension for picking colors, converting formats, and saving local history and palettes.

Summary

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Picked colors and palettes are not uploaded to our servers.
  • The Extension may send anonymous usage analytics (for example page opens and install/update events) to Google Analytics using the Measurement Protocol.
  • Color history, palettes, and preferences stay in your browser’s local storage.

Analytics

To understand feature usage, the Extension may send anonymous events to Google Analytics (property measurement ID used by the developer). Events can include which Extension page was opened (popup, options, image picker, gradient maker) and install/update signals. Events use a random client identifier stored locally; they do not include picked color values, palette names, or webpage content you browse.

Data the Extension stores locally

To provide its features, the Extension may store the following on your device using the browser’s extension storage API:

  • Current selected color values
  • Recent color history
  • Named palettes you create
  • Settings such as preferred color format, auto-copy, and history limit
  • Anonymous analytics client/session identifiers used only for usage measurement

This data never leaves your browser for Extension functionality. It is not sold, shared, or transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

  • storage — required to save history, palettes, settings, and local analytics identifiers on your device.
  • On Firefox, clipboardWrite may be used so one-click copy works reliably.
  • Host access to www.google-analytics.com — used only to send anonymous usage events via the Google Analytics Measurement Protocol.

The Extension does not inject content scripts into websites.

Clipboard

When you copy a color (or when auto-copy is enabled), the Extension writes the selected color string to your system clipboard. Clipboard contents are not uploaded or logged by the Extension.

Network activity

Core features do not make network requests. The Extension does not phone home and does not load remote code.

Children’s privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Material changes that affect data handling will also be reflected in the Chrome Web Store Privacy practices tab.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support contact listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for WhatColor - Color Picker & Eyedropper.
